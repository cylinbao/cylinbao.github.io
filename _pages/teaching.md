### University Courses
I served as a teaching assistant for the following courses at UW and NYCU, and as a guest lecturer for Systems for Machine Learning.

At University of Washington:
* [Systems for Machine Learning](https://courses.cs.washington.edu/courses/cse599k/24au/), CSE 599K, Fall 2024 (Prof. Arvind Krishnamurthy)
    * Taught three guest lectures on LLM performance optimization and ML hardware.
    * Designed an assignment on attention performance analysis.
* High-Performance Scientific Computing, AMATH 483/583, Spring 2024 (Prof. Kenneth Roche)
    * Held office hours and graded assignments for a course covering pthreads, multiprocessing, MPI, and CUDA.
* Computer Architecture II, CSE 470, Spring 2022

At National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University:
* Computer Architecture (graduate), Fall 2016
* Computer Organization (undergraduate), Spring and Fall 2015
    * Designed projects on accelerating image processing and dense/sparse neural networks through software–hardware co-design, using RISC-V, Multi2Sim, and CUDA.

### Mentoring
I find great joy in helping junior students develop skills and achieve their goals. I am fortunate to have mentored the following wonderful students.

* (Fall 2024 - Spring 2025) [Yiyu Liu](https://lau.yeeyu.org/) (SJTU), now a Ph.D. student in Harvard CS. Mentored research on lookahead retrieval for memory-efficient RAG inference ([TeleRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20969), MLSys 2026).
* (Spring 2024 - 2025) [Chi-Chih Chang](https://ccchang.info/) (NYCU), now a Ph.D. student in Cornell ECE. Mentored research on low-rank KV-cache compression ([Palu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21118), ICLR 2025; [xKV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18893), ICML 2026).
* (Summer - Fall 2023) [Yilong Zhao](https://happierpig.github.io/) (SJTU), now a Ph.D. student in UC Berkeley EECS. Mentored research on 4-bit weight-activation quantization for efficient LLM serving ([Atom](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19102), MLSys 2024).
* (Spring 2017) Jyun-Wei Pan (NYCU), now an engineer at MediaTek. Mentored research on improving SIMD-like accelerator utilization for sparse CNNs ([TVLSI 2019](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8644034)).
